    How to play

    Win the battle

    Destroy the Crimson statue on the far side of the field before they destroy yours. Miners bring gold; spend it on soldiers with the cards at the bottom.

    Your castle

    Behind your statue stands your castle. Each soldier you buy is trained in his own building and marches out through the gate: giants are let out of the prison, wing archers launch from the aerie's roof hatch. Archers on the battlements shoot anything that comes close.

    Tap a building (or hover over it with a mouse) to train its soldiers and research upgrades, one at a time per building, for gold and mana: more castle archers, passive income, Rage for swordsmen, Shield Wall for Spartans, fire arrows, Giant Growth, and the wizard's, cleric's, ninja's and wing archer's special powers. Mana trickles in by itself.

    Your soldiers

    • Miner digs gold. Keep a few working all battle.
    • Swordsman is fast and cheap. Good for flanking archers.
    • Spartan carries a shield that stops arrows from the front. Holds the line.
    • Archer shoots from the back ranks. Weak if caught up close.
    • Ninja is the fastest soldier. Shinobi turns him invisible; his first blows from hiding hit twice as hard and poison. Each blow on the same enemy hits harder (Fixate).
    • Cleric heals the wounded and cures poison and fire. Keep him behind the line.
    • Wizard throws exploding blasts, calls lightning, raises an Electric Wall and sprays poison. Fragile up close.
    • Wing Archer flies over the battle on mechanical wings and shoots crossbow bolts, some of them burning. Swords and spears can't reach him; only archers, wizards and other wing archers can.
    • Knight rides a barded warhorse with a couched lance. The fastest Order unit: runs down archers and casters, but Spartans stop his charge cold.
    • Giant smashes whole groups. Slow and expensive.

    Three empires

    Pick your side on the title screen: the Order Empire, the Chaos Empire or the Wildlands (fast Sylvan Rangers, Druids who heal and summon Treants, soldiers who regrow their wounds). In Skirmish, Choose the enemy picks who you fight. Story: The River War is five missions on Riverlands. After an offline battle, Watch replay shows it again.

    Your general

    Each army takes the field with a hero: the Order General or the Chaos Warlord. Kills level him up to level 5. Soldiers near him hit harder and keep their nerve. His two abilities sit in the bar above the dock (keys T and Y): Rally Cry heals and rallies the troops around him, Shield Wall cuts the damage they take, the Warlord's Meteor smashes the thickest enemy crowd. If he falls he is back 90 seconds later.

    Commands

    Shift + right-click queues waypoints: a dotted path shows the march on the ground and on the map. U and I use the selected regiments' specials (Rage, Vanish, Mass Heal, Spur). Ctrl + 1–9 makes a control group; the group bar at the bottom left picks them. Alerts on the right show what happened where: click one (or press L) to look. P pings the map for a 2v2 teammate. Hover over a soldier to see his health, armour and what he is strong and weak against. On a phone, hold a finger on the field for a radial menu of orders.

    Orders

    Defend forms ranks by your statue. Hold line forms them where the army stands. Charge sends everyone at the enemy; soldiers who sprint in hit harder.

    Tap one of your soldiers to command his whole regiment: tap the ground to march there, tap an enemy to attack, or use the buttons that appear.

    Tactics

    Attacks from the side or rear do more damage and break morale. Soldiers fighting downhill hit harder. A regiment that loses heart runs back to the statue.

    Camera

    Drag to move. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Twist two fingers to turn, or drag two fingers up and down to tilt. Double-tap the ground to zoom in there. Tap the map at the top to jump across the field.
